    Rule book

    The goal

    By day, gather wood and stone and build. At night, zombies come until dawn. Survive 5 nights to win. The last night ends with a boss, The Grave Titan. Endless mode has no finish line: see how many days you can last. Your best days survived can go on the leaderboard.

    Controls

    • WASDMove
    • SpaceSwing your weapon (or click)
    • EChop trees, mine rocks, feed a fire, break your own walls (hold)
    • 1Build a campfire (6 wood)
    • 2Build a barricade where your cursor points (2 wood)
    • 3Stone wall: much stronger (3 stone)
    • 4Gate: you walk through, zombies can't (4 stone, 2 wood)
    • CCraft a sword: Stone Sword, then Obsidian Sword
    • BUse a bomb if you have one
    • Esc / PPause or resume

    Staying alive

    A lit campfire burns zombies that come close and slowly heals you. It runs out of fuel, so hold E next to it to feed it with wood. Stay near it and build walls around it. You can break your own walls with E if you trap yourself. Zombies sometimes drop medkits. Zombies spawn all over the map, so a corner is not safe.

    Zombies

    Green zombies are the weakest. Every night they get faster and stronger, and their colors change: yellow, orange, red, purple, black, then ghost-white.

    • SpitterFrom day 4. Stays back and shoots acid. Walls block the acid.
    • BloaterFrom day 6. Big and slow. Swells and explodes when it reaches you, and blows up when killed. Back away.
    • RusherFrom day 7. Very fast but fragile. Walls stop it.
    • PhantomFrom day 8. Walks through walls. A lit campfire holds it back, but 3 or more swarm through. Kill them early.
    • HowlerFrom day 10. Speeds up the zombies near it. Kill it first.

    Bosses

    Endless mode has a boss every 10 days, each with its own ability. After day 100 they repeat, tougher each time.

    • Day 10The Hollow King charges at you
    • Day 20The Acidmother spits a fan of acid
    • Day 30The Warden fires a laser. Step aside when it locks on
    • Day 40The Brood Queen summons zombies
    • Day 50The Grave Titan slams the ground in a ring
    • Day 60The Pale Stalker teleports next to you
    • Day 70The Ember Prophet rains fire
    • Day 80The Bone Spinner has orbiting blades
    • Day 90The Winter Wraith chills you and charges
    • Day 100The Hollow God uses many tricks

    Dawn rewards

    Each time you survive a night, pick 1 of 3 rewards. They work for every weapon. Upgrades stop being offered once they hit their limit. Two special rewards: a Second Chance brings you back once if you would die, and a rare Bomb (press B) wipes out every zombie on the map but not bosses.

    Saving, coins and the leaderboard

    You earn 10 coins per night survived, plus boss bonuses. Spend coins on skins. Endless mode saves your run automatically: use Continue on the start screen to pick it up, or Quit without saving in the pause menu to throw the run away. Choose a username on the start screen. Nobody else can take it, and you can change it once a week. With no username you show up as Anonymous. Only fully survived days count on the leaderboard.
